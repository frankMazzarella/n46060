Planning aid. The pilot in command owns the final calculation. Empty weight and moment are N46060's own figures. The station lines and both envelopes were read off photos of the handbook graphs, so each value is good to roughly one or two percent. Run a couple of loadings on the book's own graphs and compare before you lean on this.

The handbook loading graph draws each station as a straight line from zero, so a station's moment is its weight times a fixed arm (inches aft of datum). Moment/1000 is what the handbook envelope plots. Below 1,500 lb the limits are extended at the same arms. Fuel is figured at the weight per gallon in Aircraft data.
